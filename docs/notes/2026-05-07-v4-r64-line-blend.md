# v4 R64 Closing-Line Blend -- Findings

**Date:** 2026-05-07
**Branch:** `feat/v4-r64-line-blend`
**Verdict:** **FAIL.** Best cell (hard, sigma=12) total = 2071 vs canonical
v4+v8 baseline 2069 -- **delta +2 brkt pts over 22 LOSO seasons**, well
below the +10 MARGINAL bar. Mean-mode cell delta exactly 0. The R64
LL improvement (+0.012 from the Vegas audit) does NOT transfer to
bracket points at the cheap apply-time level.
**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-05-07-v4-r64-line-blend-design.md`
**Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-05-07-v4-r64-line-blend.md`
**Strategy frame:** `docs/notes/2026-05-07-v4-kaggle-gap-strategy.md`

## TL;DR

Cheap apply-time experiment overriding v4's 32 R64 pairwise
probabilities per tournament with Vegas closing-line implied probs
(at picked sigma=12), then training v8 stage-2 on UNMODIFIED v4 +
applying to OVERRIDDEN frame, scored 22-season bracket points.
Hard-mode cell scores 2071 (delta +2), mean-mode cell scores 2069
(delta 0). Both well below the +10 MARGINAL bar. Anchor invariance
PASSED exactly (v8 on unmodified v4 reproduces canonical
`pairwise_v8.csv` byte-equal: matches=True, max_abs_diff=0.0,
total=2069). The data hypothesis at the cheap apply-time level is
falsified. Per the plan's FAIL rule: close the data-hypothesis
lane; calibration-shape engineering takes Active queue #1 by
elimination. Futures-as-feature (#1b in the strategy note)
re-prioritized down -- this null result is meaningful evidence
against external-data-as-feature in general.

## Anchor

| anchor | required | observed | verdict |
|--------|----------|----------|---------|
| v8 trained on unmodified v4 + applied to unmodified v4 == canonical `output/pairwise_v8.csv` | byte-equal (max_abs_diff < 1e-9) | matches=True, max_abs_diff=0.0 | **PASS** |
| canonical baseline total brkt pts | 2069 | 2069 (FP precision) | **PASS** |

The anchor passing exactly is non-trivial -- it proves the
`train_stage2.fit_stage2(random_state=42)` path is deterministic
and the `train_stage2.build_v8_pairwise(per_game, pairwise_v4_csv,
seeds_csv, out_path)` decomposition cleanly separates training data
from apply-input data. The same machinery scored 2071 (hard) and
2069 (mean) when applied to the overridden frames. Whatever's
limiting the override's bracket-points lift, **it's not pipeline
artifact** -- it's the actual lift from substituting Vegas R64
probs for v4's R64 probs.

Picked sigma = **12.0** (true interior minimum). Note this is one
unit higher than the Vegas audit's hardcoded SIGMA=11; the audit
caveat ("SIGMA=11 may be too peaky for tournament games") was
correct, though the LL difference between sigma 11 and 12 is
0.0003 -- negligible. Picked 12 per the cheap-LL gate criterion.

## Coverage

R64 line coverage: **648/692 = 93.6%** (consistent across both
modes; identical pair index). Missing 44 R64 games over 22 seasons:
- ~2 missing/season on average; likely the same name-resolution
  misses that the Vegas audit hit (91.5% on full frame).
- 2021 (COVID-bubble) had 20 R64 games rather than 32, accounting
  for 12 of the "missing" target slots; subtracting those, the
  effective coverage on the actual played games is 648 / (692 - 12)
  = **95.3%**.

No season had < 80% coverage on R64 alone.

## What this implies for the queue

**Per the plan's FAIL rule: close the data-hypothesis lane.**

1. **Active queue #1 (External Data: Vegas R64 line blend, Phase 1)
   is now closed.** The cheap apply-time test does not lift bracket
   points. The follow-up (Phase 2: re-train v8 on overridden frame)
   is **not** triggered -- per the spec, Phase 2 only runs if Phase 1
   is PASS or PASS-fragile.

2. **Promote calibration-shape engineering to Active queue #1.** The
   variance check (PR 30) and the two audits already pointed at
   calibration shape as the bottleneck; this null result strengthens
   that case. Concrete next experiment: temperature scaling or
   isotonic regression on a tournament-only held-out validation set,
   gated by 22-season bracket points (not LL).

3. **Re-prioritize futures-as-feature (#1b in strategy note) DOWN,
   not up.** A reasonable update from this null result: if R64
   apply-time market-consensus signal doesn't move bracket points
   despite improving R64 LL, the related "pre-tournament futures as
   v4 stage-1 feature" experiment is now lower-probability. The
   structural ceiling (#1 in the strategy note: no upset-detection
   edge; #2: stage-2 marginal value tiny; #5: v4 hedges chalk)
   becomes the dominant hypothesis for the Kaggle gap.

4. **The user's 2159/3462 Kaggle finish is, on the evidence, more
   structural than informational.** v4 has a ~17% upset-detection
   ceiling. A median Kaggle competitor that beats v4 likely does
   so by predicting upsets (not by sharpening chalk). External
   public data has been falsified (this PR) as the cheap lever; the
   remaining levers are model-class diversity (#3-5 in TODO -- MLP,
   full Bayesian BT) or structural-different signal (roster-level
   experience, injury data; lower-priority due to data sourcing
   cost).

## Caveats

- **Phase 1 is apply-time only.** v8 stage-2 was trained on
  UNMODIFIED v4 distribution; applied to OVERRIDDEN. Distribution
  mismatch is real -- v8 may downweight Vegas-derived R64 probs
  because they're out-of-distribution from its training data. Phase
  2 (re-train v8 on overridden frame) would address this; it's not
  triggered by FAIL but the fact that it COULD flip the verdict is
  a known-but-deferred follow-up.
- **The biggest hard-mode swing (2011 +5)** is the same season that
  the variance check (PR 30) flagged as a 3-of-4-metrics outlier.
  The override gains modest ground on the season where v4 was
  already bad; gains nothing on the seasons where v4 was good. This
  is exactly the pattern a "v4 needs help on its bad seasons but
  has nothing left to gain on its good seasons" hypothesis would
  predict.
- **R64-only experiment.** R32+ closing lines don't exist at
  submission time. Pre-tournament futures could in principle cover
  R32+ via per-team strength priors, but the data sourcing is
  costlier (Vegas Insider / Action Network historical archives)
  and this null result lowers its priority.
- **One-sided cell sweep.** The plan's decision matrix tested
  hard + mean. A *learned* blend (logistic of `(p_v4, p_vegas, line,
  seed_diff)`) is the natural Phase-2 candidate but is explicitly
  out of scope here.
- **Anchor cell `mode=v4-only` was implicitly run** as part of
  STEP 4 in the eval driver. It produced `pairwise_v8_r64lineblend_v4only.csv`
  byte-equal to `pairwise_v8.csv` (matches=True, max_abs_diff=0).

## What we learned (one-sentence summary)

External market consensus on R64 games -- the cheapest, most-public
external data signal we don't currently use -- doesn't move bracket
points despite measurably improving R64 stage-1 log loss. The next
move is calibration-shape engineering on v4's own output, not more
external data.
