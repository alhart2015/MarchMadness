## What this means

Per the spec's sequel-ordering matrix ("GNN fails Phase 1: Massey absorbs the structural signal"), the GNN candidate is closed. Re-rank:

- **Promote Candidate 4** (self-supervised team embeddings via regular-season margin prediction) to lead. Its saturation-break theory (latent style/matchup specificity) is similar to the GNN's "graph topology" theory but operates at the team-pair level rather than via multi-hop graph structure. Likely to face the same Massey-collapse risk, but worth scoping a Phase 1 sanity check before committing to LOSO.
- **Keep Candidate 3** (box-score four-factor distributional model) -- distinct signal class (variance distribution across games, not season aggregates). Massey-collapse risk is structurally different.
- **Deprioritize Candidate 2** (sequence model on per-team trajectory) -- shares the "already-aggregated" risk profile that the GNN failure exposes. KenPom's recent-form scalars likely absorb the same signal.

## Methodological notes

- **Per-test-season independent training.** Each season's GNN was trained from scratch on that season's pre-March-1 RS games. No cross-season parameter sharing. Phase 2 (had we proceeded) would have revisited this.
- **DayNum cutoff 120** used as the March-1 boundary; this is approximate (varies +/- 2 days across seasons depending on calendar). The cutoff was not the bottleneck -- per-season test sets had n>100, and the structural gap is far larger than any cutoff sensitivity.
- **Massey scale 0.02** tuned on 2024 late-RS data (Task 4). The verdict is robust to scale +/- 50% per the Task 4 grid.
- **Wall-clock dominated by graph-build / Massey eval, not GNN training.** Per-season GNN training was < 2 seconds on CPU (best_epoch typically at the cap, suggesting more epochs would not change the verdict at this magnitude of gap).
- **GraphSAGE encoder ignored edge attributes** (margin, site, days_from_season_start) per Phase 1's deliberate simplification. A version that consumes edge_attr might close some of the 0.10 LL gap, but unlikely to close 20x the gate threshold.

## Open questions / Phase 2 implications (now moot)

The spec defined Phase 2 (22-season tournament LOSO) as gated on Phase 1 passing. Since Phase 1 fails, **Phase 2 is not run**. The Phase 2 plan slot in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-05-09-non-tabular-model-class-scoping-phase2.md` will not be written.
